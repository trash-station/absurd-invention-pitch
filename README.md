One original sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance. The program writes that sentence to `/output/entry.txt`: "The Small-Talk Elevator narrates the weather out loud so strangers never have to make eye contact with each other again."

## Why this pitch

The annoyance is universal: the stiff silence of sharing a lift with strangers, everyone staring at the floor numbers to avoid each other's eyes. The invention fixes it by doing the social work for you — the lift itself supplies the small talk, so the passengers are off the hook. The laugh comes from the invention itself, not a joke bolted on: an elevator narrating the weather is silly, yet weirdly plausible (lifts already talk, they just say which floor you are on), and the mechanism is the fix. With the weather covered, there is nothing left to say, and nobody has to look at anyone.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
